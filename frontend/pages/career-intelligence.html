Review AI privacy settings

AI Personalization is optional. If you enable it, selected role-related information from your profile may be sent to Google Gemini to create personalized career explanations. If Gemini is unavailable, the same selected information may be sent to Groq.

Names, email addresses, school and employer names, raw project descriptions, raw responsibilities, and achievements are excluded.

AI personalization may add explanations to your three deterministic top career matches. It does not calculate or change scores or ranking. Your deterministic recommendations, detailed analysis, and roadmap remain available if you continue without AI.
